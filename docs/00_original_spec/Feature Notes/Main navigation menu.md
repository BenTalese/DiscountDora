**Desktop:**
- Always displayed (cannot hide away)
- By default the nav bar is in mini-mode (icon only)
- Hover within the navbar will expand to max-mode (icon + text)
- There's no hamburger menu icon
- In "max-mode", the navbar displays over the top of the page content (does not push the page around) https://quasar.dev/layout/drawer#example--mini-to-overlay
- The navbar has shadow of some sort to separate it visually from the page ONLY when hovered (in max-mode)
- Selecting an option from the nav bar navigates to that page and minimises the nav-bar

**Mobile:**
- There's only "max-mode" display
- The hamburger button is shown on mobile
- Clicking the hamburger button click shows the "max-mode" navbar
- The nav-bar displays over the top of content (does not push the page)
- Selecting an option from the nav bar navigates to that page and minimises the nav-bar
- The nav-bar has shadow when open to visually distinguish between page and navbar area
