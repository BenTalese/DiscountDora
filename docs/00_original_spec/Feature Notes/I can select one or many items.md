Click: opens side panel, adds stock item to selection, adds "selected" / "active" styling to item

THEN

Ctrl + click on different item: multi-select mode, adds stock item to selection, side panel switches from detail view to "# stock items selected"

THEN

Click on any item: back to single selection, side panel shows detail view

Esc: deselects item(s) and closes panel
